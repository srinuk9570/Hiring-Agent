Frequently Asked Questions

How does HiringAgent score resumes?

HiringAgent uses AI to evaluate resumes across four categories: open source (35pts), projects (30pts), production experience (25pts), and technical skills (10pts). Each score includes specific evidence cited from the resume.

Does HiringAgent offer coding assessments?

Yes! Generate customized coding tests based on the candidate's resume skills. The AI auto-evaluates submissions with detailed feedback on correctness, code quality, efficiency, and edge case handling.

Can I practice mock interviews?

Absolutely! The AI generates tailored interview questions from resume content and provides real-time feedback on technical accuracy, communication clarity, and confidence level for every answer.

How does the cover letter generator work?

Paste a job description (or just enter job title and company) and the AI generates a customized cover letter highlighting the candidate's most relevant skills and experience for that specific role.

Is HiringAgent really free?

Yes! All core features — resume scoring, coding tests, mock interviews, cover letters, and networking tools — are free to start. No credit card required.
