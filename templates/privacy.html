Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

TL;DR: Your data stays private. We don't sell, share, or monetize your information. Resumes you upload are processed locally where possible, and you can delete your data at any time.

1. Information We Collect

When you use HiringAgent, we collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Username, email address, and hashed password when you create an account.
  • Resume Files: PDF resumes you upload for analysis and scoring.
  • Job Descriptions: Text you paste for job description matching.
  • GitHub Profile Data: If you choose to connect a GitHub profile, we fetch public repository information to factor into the resume score.
  • Usage Data: Basic analytics about how you use the platform (pages visited, features used) to improve our service.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively for providing and improving our services:

  • Analyzing resumes and generating scores, feedback, and interview questions
  • Matching resumes against job descriptions
  • Fetching GitHub data to enhance evaluation accuracy
  • Maintaining your evaluation history for comparison and export
  • Improving our AI models and algorithms
  • Communicating with you about your account (e.g., password resets)

3. Data Storage & Security

We take data security seriously:

  • All data is stored on secure servers with encryption at rest
  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms (never stored in plain text)
  • Session management uses secure, randomly-generated tokens
  • We implement HTTPS for all data transmission
  • Regular security reviews and updates are performed

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • Evaluations are stored until you choose to delete them
  • You can delete individual evaluations from the History tab
  • You can delete your entire account and all associated data at any time
  • Uploaded resume files are processed and can be removed after evaluation

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. The only exceptions are:

  • AI/LLM Providers: Resume text may be sent to AI providers (Ollama, Gemini) for analysis. These providers process data per their own privacy policies.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, we may disclose information to comply with legal processes.

6. Cookies & Tracking

We use minimal cookies:

  • Session Cookies: Essential for keeping you logged in. These are temporary and deleted when you close your browser.
  • No Advertising Cookies: We don't use tracking cookies for advertising or analytics.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Data portability (export your evaluations)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below.

8. Children's Privacy

HiringAgent is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email or through the platform. Continued use of HiringAgent after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact us:

  • Email: privacy@hiringagent.com
  • Response Time: We aim to respond within 48 hours
